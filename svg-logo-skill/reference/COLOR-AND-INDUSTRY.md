# Color Theory & Industry Patterns — Full Reference

## Brand Personality → Visual Form Mapping
When the user provides brand adjectives, use this table to drive shape, color, and typography decisions:

| Brand Personality | Shapes | Colors | Typography | Archetype Bias |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sincere / Warm / Friendly** | Circles, rounded corners, soft organic curves | Warm earth tones, soft yellow, warm orange, muted green | Humanist sans, rounded terminals, medium weight, lowercase | Wordmark, Pictorial Reduction |
| **Exciting / Bold / Energetic** | Diagonal lines, acute angles, upward triangles, dynamic asymmetry | Saturated red, bright orange, electric yellow, high-contrast pairs | Bold geometric sans, wide tracking, uppercase or mixed case | Abstract Mark, Combination Mark |
| **Competent / Reliable / Professional** | Squares, rectangles, enclosed forms, low center of gravity | Blue, dark green, navy, charcoal | Clean geometric sans or slab serif, medium-to-bold, uppercase | Geometric Monogram, Wordmark |
| **Sophisticated / Luxurious / Elegant** | Thin precise lines, generous whitespace, vertical emphasis | Black, gold, deep purple (flat, not gradient), cream accents | High-contrast serif (Didot archetype), thin strokes, wide letterspacing | Wordmark, Geometric Monogram |
| **Rugged / Tough / Outdoorsy** | Angular forms, heavy strokes, thick geometric primitives | Dark earth tones (brown, forest green, charcoal) | Slab serif, bold weight, uppercase, condensed | Emblem, Pictorial Reduction |
| **Innovative / Modern / Tech-Forward** | Upward triangles, open geometric forms, asymmetric compositions | Cool neutrals + one unexpected accent (avoid blue if competitors use it) | Geometric sans, monospace for dev tools, medium weight, lowercase | Abstract Mark, Geometric Monogram |
| **Playful / Creative / Fun** | Irregular shapes, mixed geometry, rounded + angular, off-grid | Multi-color (up to 3), high saturation, complementary pairs | Rounded sans, hand-drawn/custom, varied weights, lowercase | Combination Mark, Pictorial Reduction |
| **Trustworthy / Stable / Institutional** | Symmetrical enclosed forms, horizontal lines, contained compositions | Blue, dark green, navy + white, low saturation | Serif or clean sans-serif, medium weight, uppercase | Geometric Monogram, Emblem |

**When personalities conflict** (e.g., "Sincere + Tech-Forward"): The user's first adjective is the primary driver. Use it for shape language and archetype. Use secondary adjectives for color and typography accents.

## Palette Rules
* **1 brand color + black/white** is the ideal. 2 brand colors maximum.
* **Saturation:** 50-80%. Below 50% looks washed out; above 80% looks cheap.
* **Hex format:** 6-digit hex for all brand colors. Short hex only for pure neutrals.
* **No pure black (#000)** in colored versions — use a warm/cool near-black (#1a1a2e, #0d1117).
* **No pure white (#FFF)** as a shape fill — use the background to provide white space.
* **Simultaneous Contrast:** When two brand colors touch or overlap, each shifts in perceived hue/value. Design overlap zones deliberately as a third visual element.

## Color Psychology
* **Blue:** Trust, stability, technology. Default for fintech, enterprise SaaS, healthcare.
* **Red:** Energy, urgency, passion. Default for food, entertainment, sports.
* **Green:** Nature, growth, health. Default for environmental, agriculture, wellness.
* **Orange/Yellow:** Optimism, creativity, warmth. Default for education, creative tools.
* **Purple:** Luxury, creativity, wisdom. Use carefully — flat, desaturated purple only. Purple-to-blue GRADIENTS are banned.
* **Black:** Premium, authority, sophistication. Default for luxury, fashion, automotive.
* **Pink (desaturated):** Modern, playful, inclusive. Acceptable at `PLAYFULNESS ≥ 5`.
* **Cultural awareness:** These are Western-centric. White = mourning in East Asia; purple = mourning in Latin America; red = luck in China.
* **Competitive differentiation:** The default industry color is often WRONG if every competitor uses it. Stripe chose purple in a sea of blue fintech. Standing out matters more than fitting in.

## PLAYFULNESS Color Scaling
* **1-3 (Corporate):** Monochrome or single accent. Navy, charcoal, deep green.
* **4-6 (Balanced):** One vibrant brand color + neutrals.
* **7-10 (Expressive):** Up to 3 colors. Higher saturation acceptable.

## Industry-Specific Patterns

### Tech / SaaS
* **Archetypes:** Abstract Mark, Geometric Monogram, Wordmark
* **Approach:** Geometric rigor, clean intersections, modular forms.
* **Avoid:** Circuit boards, code brackets `</>`, binary, generic "cloud" shapes.
* **Color:** Blue dominates — differentiate (Stripe: purple, Slack: multicolor, Notion: black).

### Fintech / Finance
* **Archetypes:** Geometric Monogram, Abstract Mark
* **Approach:** Stability and trust through enclosed forms, low center of gravity, precision.
* **Avoid:** Dollar signs, coins, piggy banks, upward arrow charts, shields.
* **Color:** Blue is overcrowded. Consider green, black, or unexpected differentiators.

### Healthcare / Medical
* **Archetypes:** Abstract Mark (humanist curves), Wordmark (Humanist Sans)
* **Approach:** Warmth and trust through rounded forms, open counters, generous spacing.
* **Avoid:** Plus/cross signs, caduceus, hearts, stethoscopes, DNA helices.
* **Color:** Teal or warm neutrals differentiate from dominant blue/green.

### Food & Beverage
* **Archetypes:** Wordmark, Pictorial Reduction, Emblem
* **Approach:** Warmth, appetite, authenticity. Rounded forms, custom lettering with personality.
* **Avoid:** Generic chef hats, forks/spoons, wheat stalks.
* **Color:** Red/orange stimulate appetite. Warm colors outperform cool.

### Fashion / Luxury
* **Archetypes:** Wordmark (High-Contrast Serif or Geometric Sans), Geometric Monogram
* **Approach:** Restraint IS luxury. Generous whitespace, precise letterforms. Let typography do all the work.
* **Avoid:** Crowns, diamonds, generic "premium" flourishes.
* **Color:** Black is the default. Gold accents sparingly.

### Developer Tools
* **Archetypes:** Geometric Monogram, Abstract Mark, Wordmark (Monospace)
* **Approach:** Technical precision with personality. Animal mascots are uniquely effective (Go gopher, Rust crab, Docker whale).
* **Avoid:** Code brackets `</>`, terminal cursors, gear icons.
* **Color:** Bold single colors. Avoid the purple-blue AI gradient.

### Sustainability / Environmental
* **Archetypes:** Abstract Mark, Wordmark
* **Approach:** Express values through form and color, not literal nature depictions.
* **Avoid:** Generic leaves, trees, recycling arrows, water drops, green globes.
* **Color:** Green is expected — consider earth tones or unexpected colors.
